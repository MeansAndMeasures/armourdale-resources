Geography first: Tract 426 is not all of Armourdale

Official identifier: 20209042600 (Kansas 20 · Wyandotte 209 · tract code 042600). The current Census TIGERweb polygon and the Census 2026 tract table agree on 1,918,456 m² of land, no water area: approximately 0.741 square mile / 474 acres. ZIP 66105 is a useful search term, not an equivalent boundary.

For orientation, the tract runs west of S 7th Street Trafficway, with a stepped northern edge along Kansas Avenue and the rail-side edge farther north; much of its western side follows S 14th Street, with a southwest extension. Its southern edge follows the rail alignment south of Pawnee. The geometry below and downloadable coordinates, not this street shorthand, define the tract.

Official sources: Census TIGERweb polygon query · 2026 Kansas tract table · Census geocoder. Address checks used Public_AR_Current / Current_Current. Geometry and responses were retrieved September 27, 2026.

UG Armourdale Area Master Plan / planning-team mapsApproximately 2,000-acre planning district, including much more industrial and river/levee territory. The planning description places rail tracks to the north and the Kansas River to the south. It extends beyond the tract, including east-of-7th industrial/Armourdale Parkway territory. It is a planning boundary, not a Census tract.
Real-estate neighborhood guideHomes.com groups Armourdale with ZIP labels 66101, 66102, 66105 and 66118 and discusses nearby destinations outside the tract. Its “nearby” amenities are not converted into in-tract services. A reusable real-estate polygon was not retrieved, so exact parcel-by-parcel differences remain unresolved.
Resident / organization usageARA uses “Armourdale” for its neighborhood mission while its 801 Armourdale Parkway office geocodes to Tract 9809. Residents and local press describe neighborhood relationships across institutional boundaries. This is a documented mismatch, not a reason to exclude ARA.
Nextdoor / other informal mapsArmourdale public preview exists, but its account-defined neighborhood boundary is not verified against TIGER. Platform population/homeownership summaries are not used as tract estimates.
