Armourdale · Kansas City, Kansas · Wyandotte County

Community resources,
across two tracks.

Armourdale’s Kansas River floodplain, rail yards and meatpacking/industrial history shaped both its housing risks and its working-class immigrant networks. Mexican and Mexican-American community life remains central. These conditions make bilingual navigation, food and housing support, worker connections, and environmental-safety organizing especially relevant here.

How to read this directory. Track A contains government, school, library, clinical and established institutional services. Track B contains congregation-run aid, resident networks, promotoras, organizing, informal information channels and historical discovery leads. A nonprofit can appear in both tracks when its delivery models differ. These are categories of access, not rankings. Records count programs and channels, not unique organizations. A single source is enough for inclusion.

Currency is a separate check. “Verified 2026-09-27” means public provider/agency information supports an operating service or channel as of the check; it does not mean a phone call, visit, available funding, an open bed, or every field was confirmed. “Unconfirmed” preserves a found resource when current operation, schedule or access could not be established. Dates inside sources are distinguished from crawler dates. Unknown fields remain unknown. All listed times are local Central Time.
